Pop Genre

Pop music is built for instant catchiness. It uses simple “ Verses- Chorus” structures and repetitive “hooks” to make songs easy to remember and sing along to. While classical music explores long, complex stories, pop focuses on high energy.

Famous Pop Artist

Abba, a legendary Swedish pop group formed in Stockholm in 1972 by Agnetha Fältskog, Björn Ulvaeus, Benny Andersson, and Anni-Frid Lyngstad. She achieved global fame after winning the 1974 Eurovision Song Contest with the track Waterloo, and subsequently went on to take the world by storm with the hit "Dancing Queen." One cool fact about Abba is that they were the first group to have their music video played on MTV. They are one of the best-selling music artists of all time, having sold over 380 million records worldwide.
