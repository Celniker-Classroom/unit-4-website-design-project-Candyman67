Classical Genre

A classical genre is often described as a living map of human emotions, because it uses complexes arrangements to express feeling that words sometimes can’t be captured. Unlike modern songs that usually follow a predictable verse, a classical piece is designed to evolve, taking a simple melody and transforming it through different instruments and rhythms.

Famous Classical Composer

Ludwig van Beethoven was a German composer and pianist born on December 16, 1770. He is one of the greatest composers in the history of Western music. Beethoven created numerous significant works, including symphonies, concertos, piano sonatas, and string quartets. His compositions have had a significant impact on classical music and are celebrated for their emotional depth and technical skill. In his late twenties, Beethoven started experiencing this hearing loss, and by his mid-forties, he was almost completely deaf. Despite this significant challenge, he continued to compose music. He wrote and created many of his most famous compositions during this time. His ability to produce powerful music despite such adversity highlights his immense talent and determination.
