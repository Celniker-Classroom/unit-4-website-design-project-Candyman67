Classical Genre

A classical genre is often described as a living map of human emotions, because it uses complexes arrangements to express feeling that words sometimes can’t be captured. Unlike modern songs that usually follow a predictable verse, a classical piece is designed to evolve, taking a simple melody and transforming it through different instruments and rhythms.

Famous Classical Composer

Ludwig van Beethoven born in December 16,1770 was a German composer and pianist. He is widely considered one of the greatest composers in the history of Western music. Beethoven's compositions include symphonies, concertos, piano sonatas, and string quartets, which have had a profound influence on classical music and continue to be celebrated for their emotional depth and technical brilliance. Also Ludwig van Beethoven was widely known for becoming a deaf composer, and still writing music even though he was deaf. He started losing his hearing in his late 20s, and by the time he was in his mid-40s, he was almost completely deaf. Despite this significant challenge, Beethoven continued to compose music, and some of his most famous works were created during the period when he was experiencing hearing loss. His ability to create such powerful and enduring music while facing such adversity is a testament to his genius and determination.
